What changes when you correct a result?

The exact endpoint is corrected immediately. The engine also learns from all verified corrections to improve similar predictions. It keeps human labels and rejects shared-model updates that lower accuracy on those reviewed examples.

Automatic comparisons are suggestions, not ground truth. Only your corrections train the model.
